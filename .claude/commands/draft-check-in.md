---
description: Draft a short check-in message to an athlete whose wellness or load has been flagged, or whose form is missing.
---

1. Run `npm run athletes -- athlete "<name>" --json` and read the last week of wellness, the load and any notes.
2. Write `drafts/check-in-<code>-<date>.md`: two or three sentences, friendly, naming what you noticed in their words ("you said you slept five hours and the hip is tight") and asking them to grab the physio or S&C coach before training.
3. No numbers about ratios or scores in a message to an athlete. Never diagnose. Never send it.
