---
description: Everything on one athlete: today's status, load, the last week of wellness, injuries, testing and notes.
---

1. Run `npm run athletes -- athlete "<name or code>" --json`. Part of a name works; if more than one matches, ask which.
2. Write it as a one-page profile: status today, load against their normal, the wellness trend, injury history with days lost, latest tests and what changed, and recent notes.
3. Health information stays with the people who need it. Do not paste a profile into anything outside this folder unless the operator says who it is for.
