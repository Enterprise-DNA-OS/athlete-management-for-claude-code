---
description: Everything that wants a decision today, worst first: rules breached, expected returns passed, wellness flags and missing forms, load spikes and drops, test results gone backwards.
---

1. Run `npm run athletes -- attention --json`.
2. Read it in order:
   - **Rules** first. Each line breaks a rule in `docs/compliance.md`: a missing health consent, a concussed athlete in a match or contact session too early, a concussion cleared by someone other than a doctor, stages rushed, a third concussion in a year, staff with no current safety check working with under-18s, an injury record missing its mechanism. Say which athlete or staff member, and the one thing that clears it.
   - **Return to play.** Expected returns that have passed.
   - **Wellness.** Missing forms and flags.
   - **Load.** Spikes, jumps, under-loading.
   - **Testing.** Results down by more than the smallest worthwhile change.
3. For each item give the action and the owner (medical, performance, the manager, the athlete).
4. Anything that needs a message is drafted to `drafts/`, never sent.

If the operator asks "what should I do today", pick the top three and say why those three.
