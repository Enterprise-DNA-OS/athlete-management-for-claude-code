---
description: Draft the return-to-play plan for an injured athlete (and their parents, for under-18s): the stages, what each allows, the earliest dates and who signs off.
---

1. Run `npm run athletes -- injury <INJ-number> --json` and `npm run athletes -- athlete "<name>" --json`.
2. Write `drafts/rtp-<INJ-number>.md` in plain words for the athlete: what happened, where they are now, the stages still to go and what each lets them do, the earliest contact and play dates, and that the doctor clears them before they play.
3. For an athlete under 18, address it to the athlete and their parent or guardian.
4. Never promise a date. Dates are "not before", and the doctor decides. Never send it.
